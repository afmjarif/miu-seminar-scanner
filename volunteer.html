📱 Volunteer Scanner & Event Guide

Welcome to the Tech Team! Your role is the most critical part of the event. You are the gatekeepers. Please read this guide carefully.

🟢 1. How to Start

Before the event, ensure you have:

  • A fully charged smartphone.
  • Active Internet connection (mobile data).
  • Google Chrome browser (recommended).

Steps to start:

  • Open this link: afmjarif.github.io/miu-seminar-scanner/
  • Pro Tip: Tap the 3 dots in Chrome and select "Add to Home Screen". It will act like a real app.
  • Select your assigned mode (e.g., ENTRY, FOOD).
  • Tap the big SCAN button and Allow Camera Access.
🕹️ 2. The 5 Modes

Always tap the correct button at the top of the screen before scanning.

🛡️
ENTRY Main gate. Marks them as "Entered" and records entry time.
🍽️
FOOD Lunch counter. Marks that they received lunch.
☕
SNACKS Evening snack counter.
🎁
GIFT ID Card / Gift distribution desk.
ℹ️
INFO Shows participant details without marking them for anything.
📷 3. How to Scan
  • Point the camera at the participant's QR code (on their phone or printed Event Pass).
  • Hold the phone steady.
  • When it scans, the phone will vibrate, and a pop-up card will slide up from the bottom.
  • Read the result on the pop-up card.
🚦 4. Understanding Results
🟢 VERIFIED The scan was successful. Let them pass / give them the food/gift.
🟡 ALREADY DONE They have already scanned for this mode. Do not let them pass or give another item.
Example: If you scan for FOOD and it says "ALREADY DONE", they already ate.
🟡 PAYMENT PENDING They have not paid or their payment is not verified. Do not let them in. Send them to the Registration Desk.
🔴 INVALID QR The QR code is fake, damaged, or from a different event. Do not let them in.
ℹ️ INFO Shows their details. It does not mark them as attended.

After every scan, simply tap the "SCAN NEXT" button on the pop-up to close it and scan the next person.

🛠️ 5. Troubleshooting
  • Camera is black: Refresh the page and tap "Allow" on the camera permission.
  • Connection Error: Move to an area with better Wi-Fi/Data. The system requires internet to verify.
  • Name is misspelled: Tell the participant to visit the Registration Desk. Do not fix it yourself.
  • Participant lost their QR code: Tell them to check their email (Event Pass) or visit the Registration Desk with their Student ID.
⚠️ 6. Golden Rules
  • 1. Charge your phone to 100% before the event. Bring a power bank if possible.
  • 2. Always check the Mode before scanning. If you scan for FOOD when you are at the GATE, the system will mark them as having eaten lunch!
  • 3. Do not share the scanner link with participants. This is for volunteers only.
  • 4. Be patient. If there is a long line, keep your voice calm and politely ask them to have their QR code ready.
🗺️ 7. Event Flow (Who stands where)
  • Main Gate (ENTRY): 2 Volunteers (one to scan, one to assist the line).
  • Lunch Counter (FOOD): 6 Volunteers.
  • Snacks/Tea Counter (SNACKS): 3 or 4 Volunteers.
  • Gift / ID Card Desk (GIFT): 2 Volunteers.
  • Registration Desk: Seniors and Club leaders (handling payment issues, lost passes, etc.).
